Alarm Clock Planner vs DateAlarm

Both apps exist because the Clock app cannot set an alarm for a date. DateAlarm frames it around countdowns to birthdays and anniversaries; Alarm Clock Planner treats it as a full alarm clock that happens to understand the calendar.

Disclosure: we make Alarm Clock Planner, so weigh our view accordingly. Every fact about the other apps comes from their US App Store listings and public US reviews, checked on 20 October 2026. Versions compared: DateAlarm 1.94, Alarm Clock Planner 1.3.4.

Pick DateAlarm if you mostly track birthdays and anniversaries with countdowns and notes, or use an iPhone older than iOS 26.
Pick Alarm Clock Planner if you want the alarm to ring as a real alarm through Silent and Focus, with richer repeats and groups, at a lower price.

Where DateAlarm is stronger

Countdowns

Countdowns to special days and categories for birthdays and anniversaries.

Older iPhones

Runs on iOS 15 and later; Alarm Clock Planner needs iOS 26.2.

Where Alarm Clock Planner is stronger

A real alarm

Alarm Clock Planner rings through Apple's AlarmKit, like the Clock app. DateAlarm's listing describes its alerts as sound, vibration and pop-up notifications.

Repeats and groups

Monthly, yearly and every-N-weeks repeats with end dates, tags, group on and off, and a calendar view.

Price

$2.99 a year or $9.99 once, against DateAlarm's $3.99 a week, $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year.

What Alarm Clock Planner does not do

  • No wake-up missions, sleep tracking or shared alarms. Heavy sleepers may prefer Alarmy, families sharing alarms Galarm

Pricing

DateAlarm is free, with membership at $3.99 a week, $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year, and a $69.99 Premium option (US). Alarm Clock Planner is free for up to 3 alarms; unlimited alarms cost $2.99 a year or $9.99 once.

Questions

Do both apps set alarms for a future date?

Yes. That is the core of both.

Will the alarm ring on Silent?

Alarm Clock Planner's alarms ring through Silent and Focus via AlarmKit. DateAlarm's listing describes notification-style alerts and does not say.

Free for up to 3 alarms. Rings on Silent. No account needed.
