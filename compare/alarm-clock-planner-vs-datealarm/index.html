DateAlarm alternative: DateAlarm vs Alarm Clock Planner

Both apps exist because the Clock app cannot set an alarm for a date. DateAlarm frames it around countdowns to birthdays and anniversaries; Alarm Clock Planner treats it as a full alarm clock that happens to understand the calendar.*

Pick DateAlarm if you mostly track birthdays and anniversaries with countdowns and notes, or use an iPhone older than iOS 26.
Pick Alarm Clock Planner if you want the alarm to ring as a real alarm through Silent and Focus, with richer repeats and groups, at a lower price.

Where DateAlarm is stronger

Countdowns

Countdowns to special days and categories for birthdays and anniversaries.

Older iPhones

Runs on iOS 15 and later; Alarm Clock Planner needs iOS 26.2.

Where Alarm Clock Planner is stronger

A system alarm without paying

Alarm Clock Planner's alarms ring through AlarmKit on the free version too. DateAlarm lists 'Default iOS System Alarm' as a membership feature; its free version uses notifications.

Repeats and groups

Monthly, yearly and every-N-weeks repeats with end dates, tags, group on and off, and a calendar view.

Price

$2.99 a year or $9.99 once, against DateAlarm's $3.99 a week, $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year.

About Alarm Clock Planner

An iPhone alarm clock built around the calendar, for alarms you plan ahead.

  • Alarms for any future date, picked on a calendar
  • Monthly, yearly, every-other-week and custom repeats, with an end date
  • Tags like Work or Travel, and a whole group switched on or off at once
  • A month calendar of every alarm
  • 19 built-in sounds or your own audio file
  • One-tap naps, Home and Lock Screen widgets, 23 themes
  • Backed up to your own iCloud

What we saw using DateAlarm

On 20 October 2026, on an iPhone 11 Pro Max with iOS 27.0.1, on an EU App Store account.†

  • Its membership screen lists 'Default iOS System Alarm' as a paid feature: €3.99 a week, €34.99 a year or €79.99 once.
  • Without membership it alerts through notifications; with them off it says 'Alarms cannot ring when notifications are disabled'.
  • It opens as a trial version with 3 days left.

Alarm Clock Planner, same phone, same day:

  • With the app closed, the alarm rang as the iOS system alarm, with snooze and stop on the banner, the same as the Clock app.
  • Its upgrade screen, on the same EU store: €2.29 a year or €7.79 once, or carry on free with up to 3 alarms. No ads, no account.

What Alarm Clock Planner does not do

  • No wake-up missions or sleep tracking
  • No shared alarms

Pricing

DateAlarm is free, with membership at $3.99 a week, $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year, or $69.99 once for lifetime (US). Alarm Clock Planner is free for up to 3 alarms; unlimited alarms cost $2.99 a year or $9.99 once.

Questions

Do both apps set alarms for a future date?

Yes. That is the core of both.

Will the alarm ring on Silent?

Alarm Clock Planner's alarms ring through Silent and Focus via AlarmKit, free or paid. DateAlarm's paywall lists the iOS system alarm as a membership feature; its free version uses notifications.

Try Alarm Clock Planner

Free for up to 3 alarms. Rings on Silent. No account needed.
