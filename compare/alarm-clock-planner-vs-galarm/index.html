Galarm alternative: Galarm vs Alarm Clock Planner

Galarm is the closest app to Alarm Clock Planner: both set alarms for any date and handle repeats well beyond weekdays. They part ways on sharing, accounts and how the alarm actually rings.*

Pick Galarm if you want alarms that involve other people, like one group alarm for the whole family, and you are happy for them to arrive as notifications, or if your iPhone cannot update to iOS 26.
Pick Alarm Clock Planner if you want alarms that ring like the Clock app, on any date you pick, with no account, no ads and a lower price.

Where Galarm is stronger

Waking more than one person

Galarm is built for alarms that involve other people. A group alarm rings on everyone's phone before the early flight, a buddy alarm lets you set one on your partner's phone, and every alarm has its own chat and confirmations. They arrive as notifications, or as Critical Alerts if you allow them, rather than as iOS system alarms.

Every hour, in any time zone

It can repeat down to every hour, handy for medication or a glass of water, and its alarms follow you when you fly to another time zone.

Older iPhones

Galarm runs on iOS 15.6 and later, so it still works on a phone that cannot update to iOS 26. That is also why it rings through notifications: apps could not set real alarms until iOS 26.

Where Alarm Clock Planner is stronger

It rings like the Clock app

An alarm only has one job. Alarm Clock Planner uses AlarmKit, the alarm system Apple opened to apps in iOS 26, so it rings full screen through Silent, Focus and a locked phone, the same way the Clock app does. Galarm alerts through notifications, asks for Critical Alerts (Apple's channel for urgent health and safety warnings) to get past the Silent switch, and some recent reviews say its alarms stayed quiet on Silent or with the screen locked.

Your alarms stay yours

No sign-in, no phone number and no ads. Your alarms live on your phone and in your own iCloud backup. Galarm signs you in with your phone number, keeps your alarms on its servers and, to find your friends, uploads your address book (its privacy policy, version 2025.05.12).

Made for a long list

Work, school runs and the odd trip each get a tag. A tag can have its own sound and snooze length, switches off in one tap for a holiday, and the month calendar shows every alarm at a glance.

Less to pay

Unlimited alarms are $9.99 once, or $2.99 a year. Galarm Premium is $9.99 a year or $24.99 once.

What recent reviews of Galarm mention

From US App Store reviews of Galarm read on 20 October 2026 (most recent and most helpful). These are individual experiences, not tests we ran, but these themes came up more than once:

  • ads in the free version, sometimes shown when adding or editing an alarm
  • a limit of 64 scheduled reminders once you put all your alarms in it
  • alarms not sounding on Silent or with the screen locked
  • snooze that cannot be switched off for an alarm

Pricing

Galarm is free with ads, according to recent reviews, with Premium at $0.99 a month, $9.99 a year or $24.99 once (US). Alarm Clock Planner is free for up to 3 alarms; unlimited alarms cost $2.99 a year or $9.99 once.

Questions

Can both apps set alarms for a specific date?

Yes. Both let you pick any future date and repeat monthly or yearly.

Do I need an account?

Galarm signs you in with your phone number and keeps your alarms on its servers. Alarm Clock Planner needs no account; alarms stay on your phone and in your own iCloud backup.

Which one can share alarms?

Galarm, as notifications rather than iOS system alarms. Alarm Clock Planner does not share alarms.
