Alarm Clock Planner vs Galarm

Galarm is the closest app to Alarm Clock Planner: both set alarms for any date and handle repeats well beyond weekdays. They part ways on sharing, accounts and how the alarm actually rings.

Disclosure: we make Alarm Clock Planner, so weigh our view accordingly. Every fact about the other apps comes from their US App Store listings and public US reviews, checked on 20 October 2026. Versions compared: Galarm 11.6.3, Alarm Clock Planner 1.3.4.

Pick Galarm if you want to share alarms with family or a team, need hourly repeats, or still use an older iPhone.
Pick Alarm Clock Planner if you want alarms that ring as system alarms through Silent and Focus, with no account, no ads and a lower price.

Where Galarm is stronger

Sharing

Group alarms that ring for everyone, buddy alarms for someone else, confirmations and a chat per alarm. Alarm Clock Planner has no sharing.

Hourly repeats and time zones

Galarm repeats down to every hour and follows time zone changes.

Older iPhones and syncing

It runs on iOS 15.6 and later and keeps alarms in its cloud when you change phones.

Where Alarm Clock Planner is stronger

Rings as a system alarm

Built on Apple's AlarmKit, so alarms ring through Silent, Focus and a locked screen like the Clock app's. Some recent Galarm reviews report alarms not sounding on Silent or when locked.

No ads, no account

No sign-in, no phone number, alarms stay on the device. Recent Galarm reviews describe ads in the free version.

Organising by tag

Tags with their own sound and snooze length, a whole group switched off at once, and a month calendar of every alarm.

Price

$9.99 once for unlimited alarms, against Galarm's $24.99 lifetime option.

What Alarm Clock Planner does not do

  • No wake-up missions, sleep tracking or shared alarms. Heavy sleepers may prefer Alarmy, families sharing alarms Galarm

What recent reviews of Galarm mention

From US App Store reviews of Galarm read on 20 October 2026 (most recent and most helpful). These are individual experiences, not tests we ran, but these themes came up more than once:

  • ads in the free version, sometimes shown when adding or editing an alarm
  • a limit of 64 scheduled reminders once you put all your alarms in it
  • alarms not sounding on Silent or with the screen locked
  • snooze that cannot be switched off for an alarm

Pricing

Galarm is free, with Premium at $0.99 a month, $9.99 a year or $24.99 once (US). Alarm Clock Planner is free for up to 3 alarms; unlimited alarms cost $2.99 a year or $9.99 once.

Questions

Can both apps set alarms for a specific date?

Yes. Both let you pick any future date and repeat monthly or yearly.

Do I need an account?

Galarm signs you in with your phone number. Alarm Clock Planner needs no account at all.

Which one can share alarms?

Galarm. Alarm Clock Planner keeps alarms on your own phone and does not share them.
