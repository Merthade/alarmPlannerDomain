1. Alarm Clock Planner

By Emils Ozols (that's us) · Best for: Alarms on specific dates, and anyone with a long list of alarms · 4.8★ average US rating

An alarm clock built around the calendar. You pick the day, months ahead if you need to, and the alarm waits for it. Repeats go beyond weekdays (monthly, yearly, every other week, with an end date), alarms can be grouped by tag and switched on or off by group, and it uses Apple's own alarm system, so it rings like the built-in Clock app even when the app has been closed for weeks.

Pros

  • Alarms on any date, months or years ahead
  • Monthly, yearly and every-N-weeks repeats
  • Groups you can switch on or off at once
  • Rings like the Clock app, even with the app closed

Cons

  • No wake-up missions or sleep tracking
  • No shared alarms

2. Clock (built into iPhone)

By Apple · Best for: Simple weekday alarms, at no cost · 2.5★ average US rating

The alarm everyone already has. It is reliable, free and tied into the Sleep schedule in Health and the Apple Watch. Its limit is the calendar: an alarm is a time plus, optionally, days of the week, so there is no way to set one for the 23rd, for the first of every month, or for every other Friday.

Pros

  • Free and already installed
  • Rings through Silent and Focus
  • Sleep schedule and Apple Watch integration
  • Siri can set alarms

Cons

  • No dates: an alarm is a time plus days of the week
  • No monthly, yearly or every-other-week repeats
  • No groups or tags for a long list

Questions

What is the best alarm clock app for iPhone?

It depends on the job. For alarms on specific dates and long alarm lists, Alarm Clock Planner (our app). For heavy sleepers, Alarmy or Wayk. For sharing alarms with family, Galarm. For a simple daily wake-up at no cost, the built-in Clock app.

Do third-party alarm apps ring on Silent?

Apps built on Apple's AlarmKit (iOS 26 and later) ring like the Clock app, through Silent and Focus, without the app staying open. Apps that rely on notifications or on staying open can be silenced by the Silent switch or Do Not Disturb, and several listings say so.

Which iPhone alarm apps can set an alarm for a specific date?

From this list: Alarm Clock Planner, Galarm and DateAlarm. The built-in Clock app cannot; its alarms repeat by weekday only.
