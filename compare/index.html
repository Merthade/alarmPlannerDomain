Best Alarm Clock Apps for iPhone (2026): 11 Compared

There is no single best alarm app, because people want different things from one. Some need to be dragged out of bed, some need an alarm three months from now, some just want a clock on the nightstand. Here are 11 iPhone alarm apps, what each does best, and where each falls short. One question runs through all of them: does the alarm ring like a real alarm, the same way Apple does it with their Clock app, or does it lean on notifications and an app left open?*

1. Alarm Clock Planner

By Emils Ozols · Best for: Alarms on specific dates, and anyone with a long list of alarms · 4.8★ average US rating

An alarm clock built around the calendar. You pick the day, months ahead if you need to, and the alarm waits for it. Repeats go beyond weekdays (monthly, yearly, every other week, with an end date), alarms can be grouped by tag and switched on or off by group, and it uses Apple's native alarms, so it rings the same way Apple does it with their Clock app, even when the app has been closed for weeks.

Pros

  • Alarms on any date, months or years ahead
  • Monthly, yearly and every-N-weeks repeats
  • Groups you can switch on or off at once
  • Rings like the Clock app, even with the app closed

Cons

  • No wake-up missions: stopping is a slide, and backup alarms a few minutes apart give you a second chance
  • No sleep tracking: an Apple Watch already logs sleep in the Health app
  • No shared alarms: everyone sets their own, free

2. Clock (built into iPhone)

By Apple · Best for: Simple weekday alarms, at no cost · 2.5★ average US rating

The alarm everyone already has. It is reliable, free and tied into the Sleep schedule in Health and the Apple Watch. Its limit is the calendar: an alarm is a time plus, optionally, days of the week, so there is no way to set one for the 23rd, for the first of every month, or for every other Friday.

Pros

  • Free and already installed
  • Rings through Silent and Focus
  • Sleep schedule and Apple Watch integration
  • Siri can set alarms

Cons

  • No dates: an alarm is a time plus days of the week
  • No monthly, yearly or every-other-week repeats
  • No groups or tags for a long list

3. Galarm

By Acintyo, Inc. · Best for: Sharing alarms with family, friends or a team · 4.7★ average US rating

A social alarm and reminder app. Its standout is sharing: group alarms that ring for everyone at once, buddy alarms you set for someone else, and a chat on each alarm. Each one arrives as a notification rather than a native alarm, so everyone needs Galarm installed and its notifications switched on for it to sound. It also handles any date and repeats from hourly to yearly. You sign in with your phone number, and your alarms live on Galarm's servers.

Pros

  • Share alarms with contacts, with confirmations and chat, delivered as notifications rather than native alarms
  • Any date, and repeats from hourly to yearly
  • Alarms follow time zone changes

Cons

  • Does not use Apple's native alarms: it rings through notifications, or Critical Alerts if you allow them, and some reviewers report it making no sound, or a few short beeps, on Silent
  • As a notification app it is held to iOS's limit of 64 scheduled notifications, which a reviewer ran into
  • Signs you in with your phone number and keeps your alarms on its servers; to find your contacts it uploads your address book
  • Recent reviews describe ads in the free version, shown when adding or editing alarms

It rings as Apple's native alarm first, but when we dismissed it, its own page rang again, a dancing cat between two ads, followed by a full-screen ad. The free version carries ads, and the yearly plan is $59.99.

Questions

What is the best alarm clock app for iPhone?

It depends on the job. For alarms on specific dates and long alarm lists, Alarm Clock Planner (our app). For heavy sleepers, Alarmy or Wayk. For sharing alarms with family, Galarm, as long as everyone installs it and turns its notifications on. For a simple daily wake-up at no cost, the built-in Clock app.

Do third-party alarm apps ring on Silent?

Apps that use Apple's native alarms (iOS 26 and later) ring like the Clock app, through Silent and Focus, without the app staying open. Apps that rely on notifications or on staying open can be silenced by the Silent switch or Do Not Disturb, and several listings say so.

Which iPhone alarm apps can set an alarm for a specific date?

From this list: Alarm Clock Planner, Galarm and DateAlarm. The built-in Clock app cannot; its alarms repeat by weekday only.
