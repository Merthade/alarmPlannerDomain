Loud Alarm Clock alternative: Loud Alarm Clock vs Alarm Clock Planner

Loud Alarm Clock is built around one idea: sounds louder than the built-in tones. In our test the loudness came with a catch: with the app closed, the alarm played with nothing on screen to say what it was or how to stop it. Its last update was in February 2025.*

Pick Loud Alarm Clock if you want volume-boosted sounds and voice commands, and it has been working for you.
Pick Alarm Clock Planner if you want an alarm you can see and stop the moment it rings, ringing through Silent and Focus with the app closed, from an app that is still updated.

Where Loud Alarm Clock is stronger

Louder sounds

Volume-boosted tones, and a random sound each morning so you do not get used to one. Both read better in a listing than they feel at 6 AM. Louder mostly makes the jolt harsher, not the waking easier, and a different sound every morning means you never learn the one that means 'get up'. In our test it also turned the phone's volume up by itself with nothing on screen, so it took a trip to Notification Centre to find what was ringing.

Voice commands

Its listing describes talking to the alarm, if you are awake enough to.

Where Alarm Clock Planner is stronger

You see what is ringing

An Alarm Clock Planner alarm rings full screen with its name, a Snooze button and a slide to stop, the same way Apple does it with their Clock app. Loud Alarm Clock does not use Apple's native alarms, and several recent reviews describe alarms missed because the app had closed overnight or an ad was playing at alarm time.

Still updated

Updated in October 2026. Loud Alarm Clock's last update was on 15 February 2025.

A sound you know

Pick one of 19 built-in sounds, or any audio file from the Files app, and give each tag its own, so a work day and a flight day sound different. It plays full screen with the alarm's name, so you know at once what is ringing and why.

A snooze that suits you

Choose a snooze from 1 to 30 minutes, per tag if you like, and cap how many times you can snooze, which does the job of a snooze cut-off.

Dates, tags and naps

Alarms for any date, monthly and yearly repeats, tags and one-tap naps.

What we saw using Loud Alarm Clock

On 20 October 2026, on an iPhone 11 Pro Max with iOS 27.0.1, on an EU App Store account.†

  • Its alarm list has an ad card sitting between the alarms.
  • With the app closed, the alarm started playing with nothing on screen. The only sign was the iOS volume slider as the app turned itself up, and we had to open Notification Centre to work out which app it was.

What Alarm Clock Planner does not do

  • Boost the volume. Pick one of 19 built-in sounds, or import any audio file from the Files app if you want something harsher.
  • Ring with nothing on screen. Every alarm shows full screen, so you know at once what is ringing and how to stop it.
  • 0 ads, even in the free version.

What recent reviews of Loud Alarm Clock mention

From US App Store reviews of Loud Alarm Clock read on 20 October 2026 (most recent and most helpful). These are individual experiences, not tests we ran, but these themes came up more than once:

  • alarms missed because an ad was playing at alarm time
  • the app closing itself overnight, so the alarm never rang
  • alarms that did not sound on some mornings

Pricing

Loud Alarm Clock is free with ads, one of them a card in the middle of the alarm list in our test, plus a $7.99 Super Sleeper purchase (US); its listing does not say whether that is one-time. Alarm Clock Planner's free version has 0 ads and holds up to 3 alarms at a time; unlimited alarms are $2.99 a year or $9.99 once.
