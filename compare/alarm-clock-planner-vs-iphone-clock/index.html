Alarm Clock Planner vs the iPhone Clock app

The Clock app is the alarm most people already trust, and for a daily wake-up it is hard to beat. The difference shows up the moment an alarm belongs to a date rather than a weekday.*

Pick the Clock app if you set the same few weekday alarms and want nothing to install or pay for.
Pick Alarm Clock Planner if your alarms belong to dates: appointments, flights, shifts, the first of the month, or anything weeks ahead.

Questions

Can the iPhone Clock app set an alarm for a specific date?

No. A Clock alarm is a time plus, optionally, days of the week. Without a repeat it rings at the next occurrence of that time, today or tomorrow.

Is a third-party alarm as reliable as the Clock app?

On iOS 26 it can be. Apps built on Apple's AlarmKit, like Alarm Clock Planner, ring through Silent and Focus the same way the Clock app does, even when the app has not been opened for weeks. Older alarm apps that rely on notifications or on staying open do not.

Can I use both?

Yes. Many people keep their daily wake-up in the Clock app and put date-based alarms in Alarm Clock Planner.
