Alarm Clock for Me alternative: Alarm Clock for Me vs Alarm Clock Planner

Alarm Clock for Me is a bedside clock with alarms. Its listing says the alarm needs notifications to ring, and its paid plans are weekly or yearly subscriptions.*

Pick Alarm Clock for Me if you want designer clock faces on the nightstand, weather, and a shake or math problem to stop the alarm.
Pick Alarm Clock Planner if you want alarms that ring as system alarms, no ads, and a one-time price.

Where Alarm Clock for Me is stronger

Nightstand clock faces

Designer clocks, weather, a sleep timer and a flashlight.

Stop by shaking or solving

Shake the phone or solve a math problem to turn the alarm off.

Where Alarm Clock Planner is stronger

Rings as a system alarm

AlarmKit hands the alarm to iOS, so it rings through Silent and Focus with the app closed. Alarm Clock for Me's listing says the alarm will not ring with notifications turned off.

Pay once

$9.99 once or $2.99 a year, against weekly plans from $0.99 to $2.99 or $19.99 a year.

No ads, no tracking

No ads, and a privacy label with no data used to track you. Recent Alarm Clock for Me reviews describe frequent ads, some at night, and its label lists data used to track you.

Dates and groups

Alarms for any date, monthly and yearly repeats, and groups you switch on or off at once.

About Alarm Clock Planner

An iPhone alarm clock built around the calendar, for alarms you plan ahead.

  • Alarms for any future date, picked on a calendar
  • Monthly, yearly, every-other-week and custom repeats, with an end date
  • Tags like Work or Travel, and a whole group switched on or off at once
  • A month calendar of every alarm
  • 19 built-in sounds or your own audio file
  • One-tap naps, Home and Lock Screen widgets, 23 themes
  • Backed up to your own iCloud

What Alarm Clock Planner does not do

  • No wake-up missions or sleep tracking
  • No shared alarms

What recent reviews of Alarm Clock for Me mention

From US App Store reviews of Alarm Clock for Me read on 20 October 2026 (most recent and most helpful). These are individual experiences, not tests we ran, but these themes came up more than once:

  • frequent ads, including ads playing in the middle of the night
  • weekly charges starting after a free trial
  • alarms that did not go off
  • having to keep the app open for a music alarm

Pricing

Alarm Clock for Me is free, with Premium at $0.99 to $2.99 a week or $19.99 a year (US). Alarm Clock Planner is free for up to 3 alarms; unlimited alarms cost $2.99 a year or $9.99 once.

Questions

Does Alarm Clock for Me need notifications to ring?

Its listing says the alarm will not ring with notifications turned off.

Is there a one-time purchase?

Not on Alarm Clock for Me's US purchase list, which shows weekly and yearly plans. Alarm Clock Planner has a $9.99 one-time unlock.

Free for up to 3 alarms. Rings on Silent. No account needed.
