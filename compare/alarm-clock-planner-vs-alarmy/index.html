Alarm Clock Planner vs Alarmy

These two apps solve different problems. Alarmy makes sure you get out of bed. Alarm Clock Planner makes sure the right alarm is waiting on the right day. Which one you want depends on which of those is your problem.

Disclosure: we make Alarm Clock Planner, so weigh our view accordingly. Every fact about the other apps comes from their US App Store listings and public US reviews, checked on 20 October 2026. Versions compared: Alarmy 26.41.0, Alarm Clock Planner 1.3.4.

Pick Alarmy if you sleep through ordinary alarms and need a task, like a math problem or a photo, before the alarm will stop.
Pick Alarm Clock Planner if you manage many alarms across dates and want them to ring like system alarms without the app running in the background.

Where Alarmy is stronger

Waking heavy sleepers

Missions, anti-snooze and power-off prevention are Alarmy's whole design. Alarm Clock Planner has nothing like them.

Sleep features

A sleep tracker, snore detection, sleep sounds and bedtime reminders. Alarm Clock Planner has none of these.

Reach

32 languages, older iPhones (iOS 17 and later) and about 250,000 US ratings.

Where Alarm Clock Planner is stronger

Alarms on dates

Pick any day on a calendar, with monthly, yearly and every-other-week repeats. Alarmy's listing does not describe alarms for a specific calendar date.

Rings without running in the background

Alarm Clock Planner uses Apple's AlarmKit, so iOS itself rings the alarm through Silent and Focus. Several recent Alarmy reviews describe battery drain from keeping the app running in the background.

Organising many alarms

Tags, group on and off, bulk edit and a calendar view.

Price

$2.99 a year or $9.99 once, against Alarmy plans listed from $4.99 to $69.99.

What Alarm Clock Planner does not do

  • No wake-up missions or sleep tracking
  • No shared alarms

What recent reviews of Alarmy mention

From US App Store reviews of Alarmy read on 20 October 2026 (most recent and most helpful). These are individual experiences, not tests we ran, but these themes came up more than once:

  • alarms that occasionally fail to go off
  • heavy battery drain from the app running in the background
  • a Live Activity that stays in the Dynamic Island
  • subscription prompts appearing while half awake, including an accidental purchase

Pricing

Alarmy is free with Premium plans listed on the US App Store from $4.99 to $69.99 and a 7-day free trial. Alarm Clock Planner is free for up to 3 alarms; unlimited alarms cost $2.99 a year or $9.99 once.

Questions

Does Alarm Clock Planner have missions like Alarmy?

No. If you sleep through alarms and need a puzzle or a photo to switch one off, Alarmy is the better choice.

Can Alarmy set an alarm for a specific date?

Its App Store listing does not describe date-based alarms; it focuses on wake-up missions and sleep features.

Which one uses less battery?

Alarm Clock Planner does not run in the background: iOS schedules and rings its alarms through AlarmKit. Recent Alarmy reviews mention battery drain from the app staying active.
