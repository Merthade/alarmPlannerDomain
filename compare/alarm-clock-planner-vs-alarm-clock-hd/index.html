Alarm Clock HD alternative: Alarm Clock HD vs Alarm Clock Planner

Alarm Clock HD turns your iPhone into a bedside clock, and does that well. As an alarm, its own listing is open about the trade-off: for your chosen sound to play, the app has to stay open. In our test, getting to the clock also meant getting past full-screen ads.*

Pick Alarm Clock HD if you want a big clock face on the nightstand with weather and Apple Music, and you keep the phone plugged in with the app open.
Pick Alarm Clock Planner if you want alarms that ring with the app closed, on Silent and Do Not Disturb, alarms set for dates, and 0 ads.

Where Alarm Clock HD is stronger

A nightstand display

Full-screen clock faces, colours, weather, a flashlight and a sleep timer, for a phone that stays plugged in with the app open all night. The iPhone's own StandBy mode does the clock and the weather while it charges on its side, with no app to keep open and no ads.

Apple Music and Apple Watch

Wake up to songs from your library, as long as the app stays open on the nightstand all night, its listing says, and there is a watchOS app.

Languages

33 languages, the most of any app we compared.

Where Alarm Clock Planner is stronger

Rings with the app closed

Alarm Clock Planner uses Apple's native alarms, so it rings full screen through Silent and Focus with the app closed and the phone muted. Alarm Clock HD's listing says it has to stay open for your sound; closed, it falls back to a separate notification sound, and it will not play properly when muted, on Do Not Disturb, or below 30% battery.

Opens on your alarms

0 ads, and nothing to accept before you start. Alarm Clock HD opened on a full-screen ad most times we launched it, some with no close button we could find, and in the EU it starts with a consent screen for advertising with 1,229 partners, where the only alternative to accepting is buying the ad-free version.

Dates and repeats

Any date on a calendar, monthly and yearly repeats, end dates, and tags.

Less to pay

$2.99 a year or $9.99 once. Alarm Clock HD's US list shows $1.99 a week, or $9.99 to $29.99 a year.

What we saw using Alarm Clock HD

From 20 to 23 October 2026, on an iPhone 11 Pro Max with iOS 27.0.1, on an EU App Store account.†

  • In the EU, its first screen asks you to accept advertising from 1,229 partners. The only other choice it offers is paying for the ad-free version.
  • Most times we opened it, a full-screen ad appeared within a second. One imitated a Siri message about 'junk files'. For several we found no close button within a minute and had to restart the app.
  • Its alarm editor has two sounds: one for when the app is open, and a 'Notification Sound' for when it is not.
  • It asks to use Apple's native alarms, yet its listing still says to keep the app open. The ads kept us from getting far enough to set a test alarm.
  • Its plans: weekly, or yearly after a 3-day trial.

What Alarm Clock Planner does not do

  • Need to stay open. Close it, lock the phone, put it face down: the alarm still rings.
  • Show ads or ask for ad consent. 0 ads, even in the free version, and no advertising partners.
  • Work as a bedside clock. It is an alarm you set and forget, not a display. For a clock face on the nightstand, the iPhone's StandBy mode shows one while it charges on its side.
  • Play Apple Music. Pick one of 19 built-in sounds, or any audio file from the Files app.

What recent reviews of Alarm Clock HD mention

From US App Store reviews of Alarm Clock HD read on 23 October 2026 (most recent and most helpful). These are individual experiences, not tests we ran, but these themes came up more than once:

  • the alarm occasionally not sounding, after years of working
  • long ads and upgrade prompts when opening the app
  • ads and bright screen changes on the nightstand overnight

Pricing

Alarm Clock HD is free with ads, including full-screen ones when it opens. Premium costs $1.99 a week, or $9.99 to $29.99 a year on the US list, after a 3-day trial. Alarm Clock Planner's free version has 0 ads and holds up to 3 alarms at a time; unlimited alarms are $2.99 a year or $9.99 once.

Questions

Does Alarm Clock HD ring if I close the app?

Its listing says it falls back to the system notification sound at device volume, and will not play properly when the phone is muted or on Do Not Disturb. It now also asks to use Apple's native alarms, but its ads kept us from setting a test alarm, so we could not check how it rings.

Does Alarm Clock Planner need to stay open?

No. Its alarms are native iPhone alarms and ring even if the app has been closed for weeks.
