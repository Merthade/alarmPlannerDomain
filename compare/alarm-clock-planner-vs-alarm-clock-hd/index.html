Alarm Clock Planner vs Alarm Clock HD

Alarm Clock HD turns your iPhone into a bedside clock, and does that well. Its own listing is open about the trade-off: for your chosen alarm sound to play, the app has to stay open.

Disclosure: we make Alarm Clock Planner, so weigh our view accordingly. Every fact about the other apps comes from their US App Store listings and public US reviews, checked on 20 October 2026. Versions compared: Alarm Clock HD 4.5.10, Alarm Clock Planner 1.3.4.

Pick Alarm Clock HD if you want a big clock face on the nightstand with weather and Apple Music, and you keep the phone plugged in with the app open.
Pick Alarm Clock Planner if you want alarms that ring with the app closed, on Silent and on Do Not Disturb, and alarms set for dates.

Where Alarm Clock HD is stronger

A nightstand display

Full-screen clock faces, colours, weather, a flashlight and a sleep timer.

Apple Music and Apple Watch

Wake up to songs from your library, with a watchOS app.

Languages

33 languages, the most of any app we compared.

Where Alarm Clock Planner is stronger

Rings with the app closed

Alarm Clock Planner hands its alarms to iOS through AlarmKit, so they ring through Silent and Focus with the app closed. Alarm Clock HD's listing says it has to stay open, uses a notification sound when closed, and will not play properly when muted, on Do Not Disturb, or below 30% battery.

Dates and repeats

Any date on a calendar, monthly and yearly repeats, end dates, and groups.

Price

$2.99 a year or $9.99 once, against Alarm Clock HD plans listed from $1.49 to $29.99.

What Alarm Clock Planner does not do

  • No wake-up missions, sleep tracking or shared alarms. Heavy sleepers may prefer Alarmy, families sharing alarms Galarm

What recent reviews of Alarm Clock HD mention

From US App Store reviews of Alarm Clock HD read on 20 October 2026 (most recent and most helpful). These are individual experiences, not tests we ran, but these themes came up more than once:

Pricing

Alarm Clock HD is free with Premium plans listed from $1.49 to $29.99 (US). Alarm Clock Planner is free for up to 3 alarms; unlimited alarms cost $2.99 a year or $9.99 once.

Questions

Does Alarm Clock HD ring if I close the app?

Its listing says it falls back to the system notification sound at device volume, and will not play properly when the phone is muted or on Do Not Disturb.

Does Alarm Clock Planner need to stay open?

No. Its alarms are scheduled with Apple's AlarmKit and ring even if the app has been closed for weeks.
