One Touch Alarm Clock alternative: One Touch vs Alarm Clock Planner

One Touch Alarm Clock is about speed: set the time and you are done. The trade-off is how it rings: it does not use Apple's native alarms, and some recent reviews describe the alarm arriving as a silent notification, or not sounding on Do Not Disturb.*

Pick One Touch Alarm Clock if you want the quickest possible single alarm with a fade-in, don't mind ads, and it has been reliable for you even though it is not a native alarm.
Pick Alarm Clock Planner if you want alarms that ring through Silent, Focus and Do Not Disturb, plus dates, tags and naps, with 0 ads.

Where One Touch Alarm Clock is stronger

Speed

Setting an alarm takes a couple of taps, and there is little else on the screen.

Nothing to buy

No in-app purchases are listed. It is paid for with ads instead, and it opens by asking for advertising consent.

A fade-in

Your own music starts quietly and gets louder. Since the alarm is not a native alarm, it rings only as reliably as its notifications do.

Where Alarm Clock Planner is stronger

Rings through Do Not Disturb

Alarm Clock Planner uses Apple's native alarms, so it rings full screen through Silent, Focus and Do Not Disturb, the same way Apple does it with their Clock app. Some recent One Touch reviews describe a silent notification instead of a sound.

Quick, and more than one alarm

Tap + to set an alarm, or start a nap in one tap. Add dates, monthly and yearly repeats and tags when one alarm is not enough.

0 ads, no tracking

Alarm Clock Planner's privacy label lists no data used to track you. One Touch opens by asking for advertising consent, and its label lists data used to track you.
